

Coupling and Cohesion 


Unit: Any reasonably self-contained component of a program 

Coupling: Coupling refers to the links between separate units of a program 

	- Tight coupling: Two units depend heavily on eachother 
	- Loose coupling: Two units don't depend heavily on eachother 

Cohesion: Refers to the number and diversity of tasks that a single unit is responsible for 

	- High cohesion: If a unit is responsible for a single task 
	- Low cohesion: If a unit is supposed to do many things



High cohesion and Loose coupling are the main goal.
